# 01 — Verdict (compressed, speed-run)

## Verdict: BUILD FOR SELF

The signal is obvious enough to skip the full four-artifact validation (forge speed-run rule), because the research already exists: the consensus doctrine, synthesized from ~1,100 videos across 9 corpora, is the CEP research for this exact tool. It named outlier-hunting as the most-agreed idea method (6 independent sources) and packaging-before-filming as the single most-confirmed rule (7 sources). We are not guessing at what matters; we are building the two highest-leverage functions the research already validated.

## Why BUILD FOR SELF and not BUILD FOR MARKET
- The pain is Adam's and real: he wants vidIQ/TubeBuddy leverage without the $15-40/mo subscription and without uploading his channel data to a third party.
- External market demand for "another free YouTube tool" is a red ocean. vidIQ, TubeBuddy, 1of10, Spotter, and a dozen others own that space with real teams. A solo-maintained clone has no distribution path and no moat. Capping at BUILD FOR SELF is the honest call, not a consolation.

## The gap (mandatory, even on a self-build)
There IS a gap the incumbents are structurally locked out of: **a local-first, doctrine-grounded tool that reads YOUR own research corpus.** vidIQ can't cite the AmplifyViews method or the LittleDot length study when it scores your title; ytscout can, because the doctrine lives on this machine. The incumbents optimize for their generic algorithm model; ytscout optimizes for the specific, cross-verified doctrine Adam paid ~1,100 videos of research to extract. That is not a product play (still a red ocean to sell), but it IS why the self-build is worth more to Adam than the paid tools: it encodes HIS doctrine, not their defaults.

## The one real-human touchpoint
Adam is the user and the Stage 4 field test. Not all-synthetic.
